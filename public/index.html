What is PM2.5?

Tiny bits of smoke and dust in the air. Much thinner than a hair.

PM2.5 gets deep into the lungs and blood, causing heart and lung disease.

What is AQI?

A score for the air. Low = clean. High = dirty.

  • 0–50 Good
  • 51–100 OK
  • 101–150 Bad for some
  • 151–200 Unhealthy
  • 201–300 Very unhealthy
  • 301+ Dangerous

Cairo vs WHO

Cairo's 2025 average PM2.5 was about 40.8 µg/m³, around 8x the WHO limit of 5.

Waste

28M tonnes

Egypt makes about 28 million tonnes of city waste a year, around 40% from Cairo.

Cairo 40%Rest of Egypt

Where does it go?

Around 88% goes to open dumps and only about 7% reaches proper landfills.

Recycling is growing

Recycling went from 10% in 2018 to 37% in 2024. The goal is to have 60% by 2027.
