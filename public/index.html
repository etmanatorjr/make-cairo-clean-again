What's PM2.5?

Super tiny bits of smoke and dust. Way thinner than a hair, so you breathe them in without noticing.

PM2.5 gets deep into the lungs and blood, causing heart and lung disease.

What's AQI?

A score for the air. Low is good, high is bad.

  1. 0–50Good
  2. 51–100OK
  3. 101–150Bad for some
  4. 151–200Unhealthy
  5. 201+Very bad

Egypt makes about 28 million tonnes of city waste a year, around 40% from Cairo.

Where does it go?

Around 88% goes to open dumps and only about 7% reaches proper landfills.

Recycling is going up

Recycling went from 10% in 2018 to 37% in 2024. The goal is to have 60% by 2027.
